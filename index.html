The 140th Canton Fair 2026

The world's largest sourcing and manufacturing ecosystem

Held in Guangzhou across three phases, the Canton Fair brings more than 32,000 exhibitors and 300,000 global buyers together. Here is what each phase covers.

Phase 1, 15 to 19 Oct

Electronics and appliances, lighting, hardware and tools, machinery, vehicles and spare parts, new energy, construction and industrial equipment.

Phase 2, 23 to 27 Oct

Home decor, furniture, kitchenware and ceramics, gifts and festival items, building materials, glassware, and consumer products.

Phase 3, 31 Oct to 4 Nov

Textiles and garments, healthcare and medical devices, sports goods, baby and maternity products, shoes, bags and personal care.
