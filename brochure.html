CHINA VISA OPTIONS

For Canton Fair visitors from India.
01

STICKER VISA

For direct China
  • Stay up to 30 days in China
  • Processing time 30 to 40 days
  • Allows a longer stay
  • Higher rejection risk in fair season, up to 40 to 50%
  • Best for individual travellers
02

GROUP PAPER VISA

Recommended
  • Stay up to 30 days in China
  • High approval success rate
  • Minimum 4 travellers
  • All travellers enter and exit China together
  • Most recommended for groups
03

HONG KONG TO CHINA

Most popular
  • 144 hour transit route
  • Enter Hong Kong using PAR or applicable visa
  • Enter China through a 144 hour group visa
  • Stay up to 6 days in China
  • Perfect for short business visits
OUR RECOMMENDATION
Staying up to 6 days
Hong Kong + China, 144 hour visa
Group of 4 or more
China group paper visa
Individual traveller
China sticker visa, subject to approval

Note: A China sticker visa can be processed for an additional charge. If it is rejected, a 144 hour group visa may apply with an additional charge.
