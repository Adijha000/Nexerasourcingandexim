How to Navigate the Canton Fair Without Getting Lost

Updated 12 July 2026 · 5 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To navigate the Canton Fair without getting lost, know which halls cover your product, use the official map and app, plan a daily route around booked meetings, and note booth numbers. A guide on the ground keeps you focused.

Understand the layout

The Pazhou complex is genuinely vast, spanning multiple zones connected by covered walkways, with dozens of halls grouped broadly by product type within each phase. Knowing which halls matter to your specific product category removes most of the confusion before you even arrive, and this is exactly why matching your visit to the correct phase, covered in our Canton Fair dates and phases guide, is the first step in avoiding wasted time on the floor.

Plan your route

  • Use the official Canton Fair map and app, downloaded and set up before you arrive so you are not fumbling with it on the first morning.
  • Group your meetings by hall and zone to reduce walking, planning the day as a loop rather than crisscrossing back and forth.
  • Note booth numbers in advance and take a photo of each booth as you arrive, since booth signage and hall entrances can look similar after a few hours.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and carry a power bank, since your phone's map and translation apps will be in near constant use.

Zones and hall numbering explained

Halls at the Pazhou complex are numbered and grouped into areas, with the exhibitor directory listing which hall and booth number corresponds to each company. Before your first day, pull the booth numbers for every supplier you plan to visit and sort them by hall and area, not by importance or preference. This single step turns a confusing maze of halls into a clear, walkable loop, and it is the single biggest time saver we recommend to first time delegates.

Time saving habits on the floor

Start each day at the hall furthest from your hotel or entry point and work your way back, rather than starting close and having to backtrack later when you are tired. Build in short buffer periods between scheduled meetings, since even a well planned route runs long when a supplier conversation goes well and you want to linger. Keep a paper or digital list of remaining must-visit booths that updates as you go, so unplanned discoveries do not derail the meetings you already booked in advance.

What first timers get wrong

The most common mistake first time delegates make is trying to walk the entire fair without a plan, hoping to stumble onto the right suppliers, which wastes enormous amounts of time in a complex this large. A second common mistake is booking meetings without checking they are in the same hall or zone, resulting in long walks back and forth across the complex that could have been avoided with basic sequencing. A third mistake is underestimating how tiring a full day of walking becomes, which leads to rushed, low quality conversations in the afternoon when energy is low.

A guide saves the day

Our on the ground team builds a pre planned itinerary that groups your supplier meetings by hall and zone before you land, meaning you spend your time meeting suppliers rather than searching for halls or backtracking across the complex. This pairs naturally with our guide to scheduling vendor meetings before the Canton Fair, since a well planned route only works if your meetings are booked in advance.

Resting points and recharging during the day

The Pazhou complex includes rest areas, food courts and charging points scattered through the halls, and knowing roughly where these are on your planned route lets you build in short recovery breaks rather than pushing through fatigue. A ten minute seated break with your phone charging mid-afternoon often produces sharper, more attentive supplier conversations for the rest of the day than trying to power through non-stop.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Canton Fair hard to navigate?

It is very large, but with the official map, a planned route and booth numbers it is manageable.

Is there a Canton Fair app?

Yes. The official app and map help you find halls, booths and services.

How big is the Pazhou complex exactly?

It spans multiple zones with dozens of halls, easily covering several kilometres of walking distance if meetings are not grouped sensibly.

What is the biggest time waster on the fair floor?

Walking back and forth between halls for unplanned meetings. Grouping appointments by hall and zone before the day starts avoids this almost entirely.
