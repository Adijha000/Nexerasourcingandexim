How to Navigate the Canton Fair Without Getting Lost

Updated 11 July 2026 · 5 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To navigate the Canton Fair without getting lost, know which halls cover your product, use the official map and app, plan a daily route around booked meetings, and note booth numbers. A guide on the ground keeps you focused.

Understand the layout

The Pazhou complex is vast, with many halls grouped by product type. Knowing which halls matter to you removes most of the confusion.

Plan your route

  • Use the official map and Canton Fair app.
  • Group your meetings by hall to reduce walking.
  • Note booth numbers and take booth photos.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and carry a power bank.
