Avoiding Canton Fair Scams: How to Spot the Red Flags

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To avoid Canton Fair scams, verify that you are dealing with a real manufacturer not a trading middleman, never pay large deposits to personal accounts, insist on samples, and confirm everything in writing. The fair itself is safe, but due diligence on suppliers is essential.

Common red flags

  • Prices far below everyone else for the same product.
  • Pressure to pay a large deposit immediately or to a personal account.
  • No physical factory address or refusal to share company details.
  • Vague answers about production capacity or certifications.

How to verify a supplier

  • Ask whether they are the manufacturer or a trading company.
  • Request business licence, certifications and factory photos.
  • Order samples before any large order.
  • Use secure payment methods and a written contract.
  • Consider a factory visit or third party supplier verification.

Protect your payments

Never send large sums to personal accounts. Use company bank accounts, secure trade payment methods and staged payments tied to production milestones. Keep records of every message and quote.

Is the Canton Fair safe for buyers?

The fair is well organised and safe. The main risk is choosing the wrong supplier, which you avoid by verifying manufacturers and protecting payments.

Ask directly, request the business licence and factory photos, and consider a factory visit or third party verification before ordering.
