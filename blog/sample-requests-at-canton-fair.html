Sample Requests at the Canton Fair

Updated 11 July 2026 · 5 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: At the Canton Fair, most suppliers provide samples so you can check quality before ordering. Some samples are free and some are paid, and shipping is usually extra. Always test samples before a large order.

Why samples matter

A sample lets you check quality, materials and finish in person before committing money. Skipping samples is one of the most common sourcing mistakes.

What to expect

  • Some samples are free, others are charged, especially custom items.
  • Shipping the sample to India is usually paid by the buyer.
  • Custom or private label samples take longer to produce.

Use samples well

Compare samples from several suppliers, keep notes, and only scale up after a sample and a trial order meet your standard.
