How to Follow Up with Suppliers After the Canton Fair

Updated 12 July 2026 · 5 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To follow up after the Canton Fair, contact suppliers within a few days, refer to your booth meeting, request updated quotes and samples, and start with a trial order. Keep clear records so reorders stay consistent.

What this guide covers

  • Move quickly
  • Turn talk into orders
  • Keep it organised

Move quickly

Suppliers meet hundreds of buyers. Follow up within a few days and remind them of your booth meeting, product and quantity so they remember you.

Turn talk into orders

  • Request an updated written quote and specifications.
  • Ask for samples if you have not received them.
  • Agree payment terms and lead time.
  • Start with a trial order to test quality and shipping.

Keep it organised

Maintain a simple record of each supplier, quote and sample so your future reorders are consistent and easy to compare.
