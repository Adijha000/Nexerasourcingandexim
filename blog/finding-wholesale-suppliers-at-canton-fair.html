Finding Wholesale Suppliers at the Canton Fair

Updated 12 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To find wholesale suppliers at the Canton Fair, choose the phase that matches your product, shortlist booths in advance, compare at least three suppliers per product, request samples, and confirm minimum order quantities and terms in writing.

Start with the right phase

Products do not repeat across the three phases of the fair, so pick the phase that covers your category before you plan booths or book flights. Attending the wrong phase for your product means your target suppliers simply will not be exhibiting, regardless of how well you plan the rest of the trip. See our Canton Fair 2026 dates and phases guide to confirm which phase matches your product category.

Compare before you commit

  • Visit at least three suppliers for each product so you have a genuine basis for comparison on price, quality and terms.
  • Ask about minimum order quantities and how price changes at higher volumes, since most factories offer meaningful discounts as quantity increases.
  • Request samples to check quality before committing to any order size beyond a small trial.
  • Note payment terms, lead time and packaging options for each supplier so your comparison covers the full picture, not just headline price.

Verify and start small

Confirm the supplier is a real manufacturer rather than an unverified trading company, place a modest trial order first to test quality and reliability, and keep detailed records of every quote, sample and specification discussed. This discipline protects you from the two most common wholesale sourcing mistakes: over ordering from an unverified source, and losing track of which supplier quoted what once you are comparing several at once.

Building a shortlist before you travel

The official Canton Fair exhibitor directory lists thousands of companies by category, hall and booth number, and building a shortlist of ten to twenty relevant exhibitors before you travel turns an overwhelming fair floor into a manageable, walkable plan. Prioritise exhibitors whose product range, certifications or export history align closely with what you are sourcing, and group your shortlist by hall so your first day on the floor follows a sensible route rather than random wandering.

Reading a booth and a supplier in minutes

A well organised, professionally presented booth with clear product displays, printed catalogues and staff who can answer detailed questions about materials and production capacity is generally a good early signal. Ask early in the conversation whether they manufacture in house or work through subcontractors, since this affects both pricing and your ability to control quality on a larger order. Note how directly and specifically they answer questions about certifications, minimum order quantities and lead times. Vague or evasive answers to straightforward questions are worth remembering when you compare suppliers later.

From shortlist to reliable supply chain

Finding a wholesale supplier at the fair is just the first step. The suppliers who become long term, reliable partners are the ones you follow up with promptly, test with a trial order, and build a written record of specifications and terms with over your first few transactions. Our delegation shortlists verified manufacturers matched to your product list before you travel, so your fair days are spent comparing and confirming rather than searching from scratch. Our guide to sample requests at the Canton Fair and our guide to negotiating prices cover the next steps once your shortlist is built.

Tracking your supplier comparison

Keep a simple running comparison, even a basic spreadsheet on your phone, listing each supplier's booth number, quoted price at your target quantity, minimum order quantity, and any notable strengths or concerns from the conversation. This turns a stack of business cards and mental impressions into an organised shortlist you can review clearly once you are back at the hotel each evening, rather than trying to recall details from memory days later.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find good wholesale suppliers at the Canton Fair?

Pick the right phase, shortlist booths, compare several suppliers, request samples and verify each manufacturer before ordering.

What is a minimum order quantity?

It is the smallest order a supplier will accept. Always confirm it early, since it affects your budget and pricing.

How many suppliers should I shortlist before the fair?

Ten to twenty exhibitors across your product categories is a workable shortlist, giving you enough options to compare without overloading your schedule.

Can I find wholesale suppliers without a delegation?

Yes, using the official exhibitor directory and independent planning, though this takes more time and research than travelling with pre-verified shortlists.
