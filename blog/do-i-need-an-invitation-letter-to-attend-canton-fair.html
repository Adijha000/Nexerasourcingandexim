Do I Need an Invitation Letter to Attend the Canton Fair?

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: You do not need a formal invitation letter to enter the Canton Fair. Any genuine buyer can get a buyer badge. But you do need a China visa and a Canton Fair buyer badge, and an invitation letter can make your visa process faster and smoother.

The Canton Fair is open to genuine business buyers

The Canton Fair, also known as the China Import and Export Fair, is a trade only event and is not open to the general public. But it does not require a personal invitation. As long as you are a legitimate business buyer such as an importer, trader, manufacturer, wholesaler or sourcing professional, you can register for a buyer badge, which is your actual entry pass.

What you actually need as an Indian visitor

  • A valid passport with at least 6 months validity.
  • A China visa. This is mandatory for Indian passport holders. Options include a tourist visa, a business visa, or the 144 hour transit route via Hong Kong for short stays.
  • A Canton Fair buyer badge, obtained online in advance or on site at Pazhou with your passport and business details.

Where the invitation letter really matters, your visa

While the fair itself does not demand an invitation, a China business visa application is often stronger and faster when supported by an invitation from a supplier or a delegation organiser. Many first time Indian buyers under prepare the visa, not the badge.

How to get your Canton Fair buyer badge

  1. Register online through the official Canton Fair buyer portal before you travel, or register on site at the Pazhou complex.
  2. Provide your passport and basic company details.
  3. Collect your badge, which is reusable across all three phases.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an invitation letter to attend the Canton Fair?

No. Any genuine buyer can register for a buyer badge without a formal invitation. An invitation letter only helps support your China visa application.

Can Indian citizens attend the Canton Fair?

Yes. Indian business owners, importers and traders attend every edition. You need a passport, a China visa and a buyer badge.
