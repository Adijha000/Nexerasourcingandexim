Do I Need an Invitation Letter to Attend the Canton Fair?

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

If you are planning to attend the 140th Canton Fair 2026 in Guangzhou, one of the first questions you will ask is whether you need an official invitation letter to get in. The short answer is no, but there is important nuance, especially for Indian visitors who also need a China visa. This guide clears it up completely.

Quick answer: You do not need a formal invitation letter to enter the Canton Fair. Any genuine buyer can get a buyer badge. But you do need a China visa and a Canton Fair buyer badge, and an invitation letter can make your visa process faster and smoother.

1. The Canton Fair is open to genuine business buyers

The Canton Fair, also known as the China Import and Export Fair, is a trade only event and is not open to the general public. But it does not require a personal invitation to attend. As long as you are a legitimate business buyer such as an importer, trader, manufacturer, wholesaler or sourcing professional, you are eligible to register for a buyer badge, which is your actual entry pass to the fairgrounds.

2. What you actually need as an Indian visitor

  • A valid passport with at least 6 months validity.
  • A China visa. This is mandatory for Indian passport holders. Options include a tourist visa, a business visa, or the 144 hour transit route via Hong Kong for short stays.
  • A Canton Fair buyer badge, obtained by registering online in advance or on site at Pazhou with your passport and business details.

3. Where the invitation letter actually matters, your visa

While the fair itself does not demand an invitation letter, a China business visa application is often stronger and faster when supported by an invitation, either from a Chinese supplier or from a business delegation organiser. This is where many first time Indian buyers get stuck. They under prepare the visa, not the badge.

When you travel with the Nexera Sourcing delegation, we handle your visa assistance, invitation documentation and buyer badge registration together, so you never have to decode the paperwork alone.

4. How to get your Canton Fair buyer badge

  1. Register online through the official Canton Fair buyer portal before you travel, or register on site at the Pazhou complex.
  2. Provide your passport and basic company details.
  3. Collect your badge, which is reusable across all three phases of the fair.

Pre registering online saves you long queues on arrival, which matters when a phase only runs five days.

5. Common mistakes Indian visitors make

  • Applying for the wrong visa type or leaving it too late. China visa processing can take 30 to 40 days.
  • Assuming they need a supplier invitation to enter, and delaying their trip unnecessarily.
  • Arriving without a pre registered badge and losing half a day in queues.
  • Not planning which of the three phases matches their product category.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an invitation letter to attend the Canton Fair?

No. Any genuine buyer can register for a buyer badge without a formal invitation. An invitation letter only helps support your China visa application.

Can Indian citizens attend the Canton Fair?

Yes. Indian business owners, importers and traders attend every edition. You need a passport, a China visa and a buyer badge.

How early should I start the visa process?

Start 6 to 8 weeks before travel. China visa processing can take up to 30 to 40 days, so early preparation is essential.
