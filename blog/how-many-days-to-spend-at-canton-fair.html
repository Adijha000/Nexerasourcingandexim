How Many Days Should You Spend at the Canton Fair?

Updated 11 July 2026 · 5 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: Most buyers need 3 to 5 days at the Canton Fair to cover one phase properly. If your products span two phases, plan 7 to 10 days. Add travel days and time for factory visits near Guangzhou if you want to inspect production.

One phase, 3 to 5 days

A single phase runs about five days and covers a huge area. Three to five days lets you meet your shortlisted suppliers, compare options and negotiate without rushing.

Two phases or more, 7 to 10 days

If your product categories fall in different phases, plan a longer trip. Remember there is a short gap between phases, so build that into your plan.

Add time for factory visits

Serious buyers often add one or two days to visit factories near Guangzhou and inspect production in person. This is one of the highest value parts of the trip.

A planned itinerary saves days

With meetings booked in advance and a shortlist ready, you achieve in three focused days what an unplanned visitor struggles to do in six. That is the core of how our delegation works.
