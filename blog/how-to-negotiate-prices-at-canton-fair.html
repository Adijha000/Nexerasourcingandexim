How to Negotiate Prices at the Canton Fair Like a Pro

Updated 11 July 2026 · 7 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To negotiate well at the Canton Fair, know your target price before you arrive, ask about the price at higher order quantities, request samples, and never accept the first quote. Build a relationship, compare several suppliers, and confirm everything in writing.

Prepare before you negotiate

  • Know your target landed price including shipping and duties.
  • Research typical prices for your product so you can spot a fair quote.
  • Prepare your order quantity, since price drops as volume rises.

Tactics that work on the floor

  • Never accept the first price. Ask what the price is at a larger quantity.
  • Compare at least three suppliers for the same product before deciding.
  • Ask for samples to check quality before committing.
  • Be polite and build rapport. Relationships matter in Chinese business culture.
  • Discuss payment terms, not just unit price. Terms can be worth more than a small discount.

Close the deal safely

Confirm specifications, price, quantity, lead time and payment terms in writing. Take photos of the booth, product and business card. Do not rush a large first order. Start with a trial order to test quality and reliability.

Frequently asked questions

Can you really negotiate at the Canton Fair?

Yes. Quoted prices are usually a starting point. Higher quantities, samples and payment terms are all negotiable.

Do I need a translator to negotiate?

It helps a lot. A translator removes misunderstandings and helps you build the relationship that unlocks better terms.
