Buyer badge

How to Get a Buyer Badge for the Canton Fair

Updated 11 July 2026 · 5 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To get a Canton Fair buyer badge, register online through the official buyer portal before you travel or register on site at the Pazhou complex with your passport and company details. The badge is free to obtain and works across all three phases.

What the buyer badge is

The buyer badge is your entry pass to the Canton Fair. Without it you cannot enter the halls. It is separate from your visa, and you need both.

Register online before you travel

  1. Go to the official Canton Fair buyer registration portal.
  2. Create an account and enter your passport and company details.
  3. Upload a photo and submit. Save your confirmation and badge number.

Register on site at Pazhou

If you did not register online, you can register at the badge counters at the Pazhou complex. Bring your passport, a business card and company proof. Expect queues, especially on the first day of a phase, so arrive early.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Canton Fair buyer badge free?

Yes, obtaining a buyer badge is free. You only pay for travel, visa and your own costs.

Do I need a new badge for each phase?

No. One badge works across all three phases of the same fair.
