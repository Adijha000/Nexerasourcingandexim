How to Schedule Meetings with Vendors Before the Canton Fair

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To schedule vendor meetings before the Canton Fair, shortlist exhibitors from the official directory, contact them early with clear product requirements, confirm booth numbers and times, and keep a daily plan. A delegation can arrange verified supplier meetings for you in advance.

Why pre scheduling matters

With more than 32,000 exhibitors, walking in without a plan wastes days. Booked meetings turn a five day phase into a focused, productive trip.

How to do it step by step

  1. Browse the official Canton Fair exhibitor directory for your product category.
  2. Shortlist target booths and note their hall and stand numbers.
  3. Contact suppliers by email or WeChat with your product needs and target quantity.
  4. Confirm a day and time, and keep a simple daily schedule.
