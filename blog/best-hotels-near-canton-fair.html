Best Areas and Hotels Near the Canton Fair

Updated 12 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: The best area to stay for the Canton Fair is Pazhou, right next to the fair complex, followed by Zhujiang New Town and areas along Line 8 of the metro. Book early, since hotel prices in Guangzhou rise sharply during the fair.

Stay in Pazhou for convenience

Pazhou is the district that surrounds the fair complex itself, and it is the single most convenient place to stay if you want to minimise commute time. Staying here means a short walk or a five to ten minute ride to the halls each morning, which saves real time across a five day phase with back to back supplier meetings. Hotels here fill up fastest, especially near the main entrances, so this is the first area to check when you start booking, and it is the natural first choice for serious buyers who value every hour on the fair floor.

Zhujiang New Town for comfort

Zhujiang New Town is Guangzhou's central business district, with a wider choice of quality hotels, restaurants and shopping, plus easy metro access to Pazhou, usually a ride of fifteen to twenty five minutes depending on the line and connection. This area suits buyers who want a comfortable base with more dining and lifestyle options in the evening, without sacrificing too much convenience on the commute. It is also a sensible choice if you are extending your stay beyond the fair days to explore Guangzhou or meet suppliers elsewhere in the city.

Along the metro for value

Hotels along the metro line that runs to Pazhou offer noticeably better value than properties right at the fair complex, and still keep your commute manageable if you pick a station within a few stops. Check the walking distance from the metro station to the hotel entrance before booking, since Guangzhou blocks can be larger than they look on a map, and factor in that trains run frequently but can get crowded during peak fair hours.

Book early, prices spike

Guangzhou hotel prices rise sharply during the fair, sometimes several times over normal rates, and good rooms sell out weeks in advance, particularly for Phase 1 which draws the largest number of international buyers. Book well ahead of your travel dates, ideally three to four weeks before your phase begins. Our delegation includes vetted hotel accommodation and daily transfers as part of the package, so this planning and the price risk is handled for you rather than left to a last minute scramble.

What to check before you book

Beyond location, check whether the hotel offers airport transfer assistance, whether staff speak workable English, and whether the property has hosted fair delegates before, since this usually means smoother check in during the busiest arrival days. Read recent reviews specifically from the fair season rather than general reviews, since service quality at some properties can dip noticeably when occupancy is at its peak. Confirm your cancellation policy too, in case your travel dates shift due to visa processing timelines.

Budget versus premium hotels

A mid range hotel in Pazhou or along the metro line is usually the sweet spot for most Indian buyers, balancing cost with the convenience of a short commute. Premium hotels in Zhujiang New Town offer a more comfortable stay with better amenities, at a noticeably higher nightly rate during fair season, and can make sense if you are travelling with a larger team or want a base that also works for evening business dinners. Purely budget accommodation far from both areas rarely saves enough to offset the extra commute time and transfer cost across a multi day phase, so weigh the total cost of the stay, not just the room rate.

Getting around once you have chosen an area

Wherever you stay, plan your daily commute pattern before the fair begins so mornings are not spent figuring out routes. Frequently asked questions

Where is the best place to stay for the Canton Fair?

Pazhou, next to the fair complex, is the most convenient. Zhujiang New Town is a comfortable alternative with good metro links.

Are hotels expensive during the Canton Fair?

Yes. Prices rise sharply and rooms sell out, so book early or travel with a delegation that includes accommodation.

How far in advance should I book a hotel for the Canton Fair?

Book at least three to four weeks ahead for Phase 1, since it draws the largest crowds. Popular Pazhou hotels near the complex sell out first.

Is it worth paying more to stay right next to Pazhou?

For a five day phase, yes. The time saved on daily commuting usually outweighs the higher room rate, especially if you have back to back supplier meetings.
