Canton Fair 2026 Dates and Phases

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: The 140th Canton Fair runs in three phases between 15 October and 4 November 2026. Phase 1 covers electronics and machinery, Phase 2 covers home and consumer goods, and Phase 3 covers textiles, health and personal care. Attend the phase that matches your product.

The three phases at a glance

The Canton Fair is too large to run at once, so it is split into three phases, each about five days. Products do not repeat across phases, so choosing the right phase is the most important planning decision you make.

Phase 1, 15 to 19 October 2026

Electronics and appliances, lighting, hardware and tools, machinery, vehicles and spare parts, new energy, construction and industrial equipment.

Phase 2, 23 to 27 October 2026

Home decor, furniture, kitchenware and ceramics, gifts and festival items, building materials, glassware and general consumer products.

Phase 3, 31 October to 4 November 2026

Textiles and garments, healthcare and medical devices, sports goods, baby and maternity products, shoes, bags and personal care.

How to choose your phase

Match your sourcing goal to the phase above. If your products fall in more than one phase, plan a longer stay or focus on the phase where most of your suppliers exhibit. Our team helps you pick the right phase and shortlists suppliers before you travel.
