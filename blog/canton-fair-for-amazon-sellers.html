Canton Fair for Amazon Sellers: A Complete Guide

Updated 12 July 2026 · 7 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: The Canton Fair is very useful for Amazon and ecommerce sellers who want private label products, better margins and reliable suppliers. Meeting factories in person helps you improve product quality, negotiate lower costs and build a supply chain competitors cannot easily copy.

Why Amazon sellers go to the Canton Fair

  • Private label. Find factories that customise products with your brand, packaging and inserts.
  • Margins. Direct factory pricing beats agent and platform pricing, often by a meaningful margin once middlemen are removed.
  • Quality. Inspect samples and materials in person before you list, catching issues that photos on a supplier's page never reveal.
  • New products. Spot trends and new items before competitors who are only browsing supplier catalogues online.

How to work the fair as a seller

  • Focus on the phase that matches your niche, then shortlist booths from the exhibitor directory in advance rather than wandering the halls.
  • Carry your product requirements, target price, and packaging ideas printed or saved on your phone so you can share them instantly.
  • Collect samples and business cards, and photograph everything, including booth numbers, so you can match samples to suppliers later.
  • Ask about minimum order quantities and customisation options directly, and note down what is negotiable versus fixed.

From fair to first order

After the fair, follow up quickly while the conversation is fresh, request updated quotes reflecting anything discussed at the booth, and start with a trial order sized to test quality, packaging and shipping reliability before committing to a full container or larger purchase order. Keep clear written records of specifications, colours, materials and packaging requirements so your reorders stay consistent even if you switch to a different contact at the same factory later.

Negotiating MOQ and price as a smaller seller

Many Amazon sellers worry that published minimum order quantities online are too high for a first order, but factories at the fair are frequently more flexible in person than their listed MOQs suggest, especially when you can show a credible plan to reorder at higher volumes once your listing is validated. Be upfront about being an early stage or growing seller, propose a smaller trial order at a slightly higher unit price if needed, and use that trial to build trust for better terms on the next order. Bringing a clear one page brief of your product spec, target price and expected order growth helps factories take a smaller first order seriously.

Which phase suits which Amazon category

Electronics, gadgets, tools and lighting sellers should target Phase 1, home, kitchen, decor and general merchandise sellers should target Phase 2, and sellers in apparel, personal care, baby products or sports goods should target Phase 3. If your catalogue spans categories in different phases, prioritise the phase where your best selling or highest margin products fall, and treat the fair as a way to deepen that category rather than spreading thin across all three phases in one trip.

Mistakes ecommerce sellers make at the fair

The most common mistake is arriving without a clear product brief, which leads to vague conversations that produce generic quotes instead of workable numbers. A second mistake is not asking about packaging and private label capability early, only to discover late that a promising factory cannot do custom branding at the volume needed. A third mistake is committing to a large first order without a trial run, which magnifies any quality or communication issue across an entire shipment rather than a small test batch.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Canton Fair good for Amazon FBA sellers?

Yes. It is ideal for finding private label suppliers, improving margins and building a reliable supply chain.

Can I get private label products at the Canton Fair?

Yes. Many manufacturers offer customisation and private label options. Discuss branding, packaging and minimum order quantities directly.

What minimum order quantity should Amazon sellers expect?

MOQs vary widely by category, but factories at the fair are often more flexible in person than listed online, especially if you show a clear plan to reorder.

Should I ship samples or carry them back to India myself?

For a few small samples, carrying them is simplest. For bulkier or multiple supplier samples, courier shipping consolidated at the end of the fair is usually more practical.
