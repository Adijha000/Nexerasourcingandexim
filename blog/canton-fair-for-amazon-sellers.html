Canton Fair for Amazon Sellers: A Complete Guide

Updated 12 July 2026 · 7 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: The Canton Fair is very useful for Amazon and ecommerce sellers who want private label products, better margins and reliable suppliers. Meeting factories in person helps you improve product quality, negotiate lower costs and build a supply chain competitors cannot easily copy.

What this guide covers

  • Why Amazon sellers go to the Canton Fair
  • How to work the fair as a seller
  • From fair to first order
  • Source smarter with support

Why Amazon sellers go to the Canton Fair

  • Private label. Find factories that customise products with your brand.
  • Margins. Direct factory pricing beats agent and platform pricing.
  • Quality. Inspect samples and materials in person before you list.
  • New products. Spot trends and new items before competitors.

How to work the fair as a seller

  • Focus on the phase that matches your niche, then shortlist booths in advance.
  • Carry your product requirements, target price and packaging ideas.
  • Collect samples and business cards, and photograph everything.
  • Ask about minimum order quantities and customisation options.

From fair to first order

After the fair, follow up quickly, request updated quotes and start with a trial order to test quality and shipping. Keep clear records of specifications so your reorders are consistent.

Is the Canton Fair good for Amazon FBA sellers?
