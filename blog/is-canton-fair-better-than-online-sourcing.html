Is the Canton Fair Better Than Online Sourcing?

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: The Canton Fair and online sourcing both have a place. Online is convenient for research and small orders, while the Canton Fair is better for meeting manufacturers in person, checking quality, negotiating and building trust for larger or long term sourcing.

Where online sourcing wins

Online platforms are convenient for early research, small orders and comparing many options quickly from your desk.

Where the Canton Fair wins

  • You meet manufacturers in person and see products first hand.
  • You negotiate better prices and terms face to face.
  • You reduce the risk of online sourcing mistakes.
  • You build relationships that lead to reliable long term supply.

Use both together

Many smart importers research online, then confirm and expand at the Canton Fair. Our delegation makes the in person part efficient and productive.
