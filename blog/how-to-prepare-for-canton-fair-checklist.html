How to Prepare for the Canton Fair: Complete Checklist

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To prepare for the Canton Fair, sort your visa and buyer badge early, choose the right phase, shortlist target suppliers, set your target prices, and pack business cards, comfortable shoes and a way to record quotes. Preparation is what makes the trip pay off.

Documents and approvals

  • China visa applied for 6 to 8 weeks before travel.
  • Canton Fair buyer badge registered.
  • Passport valid for at least 6 months.
  • Confirmed flights and hotel.

Sourcing plan

  • Choose the phase that matches your products.
  • Shortlist target suppliers and booths.
  • Set your target price and order quantity for each product.
  • Prepare product specifications and packaging notes.

What to carry

  • Plenty of business cards.
  • A notebook or phone app to record quotes and booth numbers.
  • Comfortable shoes, the halls are enormous.
  • A power bank and the right plug adapter.
