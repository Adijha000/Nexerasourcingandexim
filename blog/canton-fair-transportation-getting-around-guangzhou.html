Canton Fair Transportation: Getting Around Guangzhou

Updated 12 July 2026 · 5 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: Getting around Guangzhou for the Canton Fair is easy with the metro, which connects directly to the Pazhou complex. Taxis and ride apps are widely used. A delegation includes daily transfers so you never worry about routes.

The metro is your friend

Guangzhou has a fast, modern and affordable metro system. Line 8 connects directly to the Pazhou complex with stations right at the exhibition halls, and staying near a metro station on this line keeps your daily commute short and predictable regardless of surface traffic. Trains run frequently, and signage includes English, which makes the metro one of the least stressful ways to reach the fair each morning.

Other options

  • Taxis and local ride hailing apps for door to door travel, useful when carrying samples or catalogues.
  • Hotel shuttles where available, particularly from larger properties in Pazhou or Zhujiang New Town during fair week.
  • Walking within the Pazhou area if your hotel is close, which many delegates prefer for the shortest possible commute.

From the airport to Pazhou

Most international flights from India land at Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport, which sits a reasonable distance from the Pazhou complex. A taxi or ride hailing app typically takes between 45 and 75 minutes depending on traffic and time of day, while a combination of the airport express and metro transfer can be a cost effective alternative if you are travelling light. If you land during peak fair week, build in extra buffer time, since airport taxi queues can lengthen noticeably as international delegate arrivals cluster around the opening days of each phase.

Ride hailing apps in Guangzhou

Didi is the dominant ride hailing app across Guangzhou and much of China, and setting up an account with a compatible payment method before you travel saves time rather than fumbling with app downloads and verification once you land. Drivers rarely speak much English, so having your hotel address and destination saved in Chinese characters, which your hotel concierge can usually help translate, smooths out every ride. Keep your phone charged, since you will likely rely on the app several times a day for short hops between meetings.

Avoiding rush hour and crowds

Guangzhou's metro and roads around Pazhou get genuinely crowded in the hour before the halls open and again as they close each evening, especially on the first day of a new phase. Leaving your hotel twenty to thirty minutes earlier than you think you need avoids the worst of this crush and gets you into the halls with time to spare before your first scheduled meeting. If your schedule allows flexibility, starting the day slightly later, once the opening rush has passed, can also make for a noticeably smoother commute.

This is paired with hotel bookings in convenient areas, covered in our guide to the best hotels near the Canton Fair, so your daily commute is planned before you even land.

Getting between hotels and restaurants in the evening

Evenings during fair week are a good time to explore Guangzhou's restaurant scene or meet suppliers for a relationship building dinner, and the metro generally runs late enough to support this. If you plan a later evening out, confirm the last train time on your line beforehand, since a ride hailing app booked after midnight in a busy area can take longer to arrive than during the day.

Carrying samples and luggage on transit

If you are carrying bulky samples or catalogues collected through the day, the metro can get tight during peak hours, so a taxi or ride hailing app becomes more practical for that leg of your commute. Keep valuables and important documents in a bag you carry close rather than checked into a hotel shuttle's luggage compartment, especially on your final transfer to the airport.
