Canton Fair Visa Requirements for Indian Visitors

Updated 12 July 2026 · 7 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: Indian passport holders need a China visa to attend the Canton Fair. Most business buyers use a business visa or a tourist visa. For short trips you can also enter through Hong Kong using the 144 hour visa free transit. Start the process 6 to 8 weeks before travel.

Which China visa should Indian visitors choose

There are three common routes for Indian buyers attending the Canton Fair in Guangzhou.

  • Business visa (M visa). Best if you plan to meet suppliers and sign deals. Often supported by an invitation from a supplier or delegation, and generally the most appropriate category for genuine sourcing trips.
  • Tourist visa (L visa). Simpler to obtain in some cases and accepted for attending the fair on a short trip, though it offers less flexibility if your plans extend to factory visits or extended stays.
  • Hong Kong route with 144 hour transit. Enter via Hong Kong and travel into Guangzhou for a short stay without a full China visa, subject to eligibility and specific routing requirements that should be confirmed before relying on this option.

Documents you will usually need

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates, with at least two blank pages.
  • Completed visa application form and a recent passport style photo meeting the consulate's specifications.
  • Confirmed flight and hotel bookings, which most applications require as proof of travel intent.
  • Business documents such as a company registration certificate, GST certificate, or import export code (IEC) registration.
  • An invitation letter, which strengthens a business visa application considerably, especially for first time applicants.

How long the China visa takes

Plan early and do not treat this step casually. Standard China visa processing for Indian applicants can take 30 to 40 days during busy periods around major fair seasons, when application volumes surge. Begin the process 6 to 8 weeks before your travel date so you are never rushed, and so a request for additional documents does not threaten your travel plans.

Applying through the right consulate

Indian applicants generally need to apply through the Chinese consulate or visa application service centre with jurisdiction over their home state or city, rather than any consulate of their choosing. Check the current jurisdiction map on the official visa application service website before booking an appointment, since applying at the wrong centre can cause delays or rejection purely on a technicality unrelated to your actual eligibility.

Common visa application mistakes

A common mistake is submitting a photo that does not meet the strict specifications for background, size or recency, which causes an otherwise straightforward application to bounce back for correction. Another common mistake is applying for a tourist visa when the actual purpose of the trip, meeting suppliers and negotiating orders, more accurately fits a business visa category, which can create complications if questioned at any stage. A third mistake is leaving no buffer time before travel, so that even a minor additional document request from the consulate threatens the entire trip. Building in one to two weeks of slack before your planned departure protects against this.

Frequently asked questions

Do Indian citizens need a visa for the Canton Fair?

Yes. A China visa is mandatory for Indian passport holders, or you can use the Hong Kong 144 hour transit route for a short stay.

How early should I apply for a China visa from India?

Apply 6 to 8 weeks before travel, since processing can take 30 to 40 days in peak season.

Which Chinese consulate should Indian applicants use?

Apply through the Chinese consulate or visa application centre with jurisdiction over your home city or state, as listed on the official visa service website.

What happens if my visa application is rejected or delayed?

Have a buffer of at least one to two weeks before travel so you can address any additional document requests without missing your fair dates.
