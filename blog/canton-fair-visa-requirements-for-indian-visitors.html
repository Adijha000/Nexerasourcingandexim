Canton Fair Visa Requirements for Indian Visitors

Updated 11 July 2026 · 7 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: Indian passport holders need a China visa to attend the Canton Fair. Most business buyers use a business visa or a tourist visa. For short trips you can also enter through Hong Kong using the 144 hour visa free transit. Start the process 6 to 8 weeks before travel.

Which China visa should Indian visitors choose

There are three common routes for Indian buyers attending the Canton Fair in Guangzhou.

  • Business visa. Best if you plan to meet suppliers and sign deals. Often supported by an invitation from a supplier or delegation.
  • Tourist visa. Simpler to obtain and accepted for attending the fair on a short trip.
  • Hong Kong route with 144 hour transit. Enter via Hong Kong and travel into Guangzhou for a short stay without a full China visa, subject to eligibility.

Documents you will usually need

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months with blank pages.
  • Completed visa application form and a recent passport photo.
  • Confirmed flight and hotel bookings.
  • Business documents such as a company registration or GST certificate.
  • An invitation letter, which strengthens a business visa application.

How long the China visa takes

Plan early. Standard China visa processing for Indian applicants can take 30 to 40 days during busy periods. Begin 6 to 8 weeks before your travel date so you are never rushed.
