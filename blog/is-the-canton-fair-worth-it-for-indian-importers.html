Is the Canton Fair Worth It for Indian Importers?

Updated 11 July 2026 · 7 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: For serious Indian importers, the Canton Fair is usually worth it. Meeting verified manufacturers in person lets you source directly, compare thousands of factories in days, and cut middleman margins. The trip pays off when you go with a plan and booked supplier meetings rather than wandering the halls.

What makes the Canton Fair valuable

  • Direct factory access. You deal with manufacturers, not agents, so prices are lower and terms are clearer.
  • Scale. More than 32,000 exhibitors in one place means you compare options in days, not months.
  • Trust. Seeing products and meeting people in person reduces the risk of online sourcing mistakes.
  • Negotiation. In person conversations unlock better prices, samples and payment terms.

When it is not worth it

The fair disappoints buyers who arrive with no plan, no target suppliers and no badge, and then get lost in 1.1 million square metres. The cost of the trip is wasted if you spend it wandering instead of meeting the right factories.

How to make sure it pays off

The difference between a wasted trip and a profitable one is preparation. Go with a shortlist of verified suppliers, meetings booked in advance, and someone on the ground who speaks the language.
