How to Verify Legitimate Suppliers at the Canton Fair

Updated 11 July 2026 · 6 min read · By Nexera Sourcing and Exim

Quick answer: To verify a Canton Fair supplier, ask whether they are the manufacturer or a trading company, request their business licence and certifications, review factory photos, order samples, and consider a factory visit or third party verification before a large order.

Manufacturer or middleman

Ask directly. Manufacturers usually offer better pricing and control over quality than trading companies. Both can be legitimate, but you should know which you are dealing with.

Checks that protect you

  • Request the business licence and relevant certifications.
  • Ask for factory photos, capacity and export history.
  • Order samples before committing.
  • Use secure payment methods and a written contract.

Go further for large orders

For big orders, a factory visit or third party inspection is worth the cost. Our delegation offers supplier verification and factory visits so you deal only with trustworthy partners.
